The distribution you asked about

How sure are we? — bootstrap distribution of the answer

Method. Nonparametric bootstrap. Whatever the question, we compute the answer once on your sample, then resample the sample with replacement (same n) and recompute it — M×. The spread of those M answers is our uncertainty: the 95% interval is the 2.5th–97.5th percentiles. For an event like P(X > T) the answer is the fraction of values in the event; for P(mean > T) the statistic is the mean. Same machine, any question — no closed-form formula needed.
